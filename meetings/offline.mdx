With a local voice model and a local language model, the audio, transcript, and summary never leave your Mac, and meetings work offline.

## Set up local meetings

1. In the **Meetings** tab, click the gear
2. Set **Voice** to **Cohere Transcribe**, or **Parakeet** for speed over accuracy
3. Set **LLM** to **Tiny Aya 3B** or **Qwen 3.5 2B**
4. Wait for the models to download

| Model | Role | Size | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cohere Transcribe | Voice | 1.3 GB | The most accurate local transcription, in 14 languages. Needs Apple silicon |
| Tiny Aya 3B | LLM | 2.1 GB | Summaries in 60+ languages |
| Qwen 3.5 2B | LLM | 1.3 GB | Faster summaries on smaller Macs |

Local summaries are shorter than cloud summaries. See [local summary tips](/meetings/summaries#local-summaries-are-short-or-miss-the-format).

## Where meeting data goes

Speaker separation, calendar data, and saved meetings always stay on your Mac. The models decide the rest:

| Data | Local models | Cloud models |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audio | Stays on your Mac | Sent to the voice model provider |
| Transcript and private notes | Stay on your Mac | Sent to the language model provider for the summary |

Mix them to suit you. A local voice model with a cloud LLM keeps audio on your Mac and sends only text.

Superwhisper's cloud providers don't keep or train on your data. See [Cloud models: privacy commitments](/security/sensitive-data#cloud-models-privacy-commitments).

Data also leaves your Mac when you:

- **Create a share link**: uploads the summary text, title, and date for 30 days. See [Share a link](/meetings/share#share-a-link)
- **Connect an agent**: the agent's provider receives what it reads. See [Ask an agent](/meetings/share#ask-an-agent-about-your-meetings)
- **Drag or copy a summary**: the receiving app's terms apply

## Recording consent

Many places require consent from everyone on a call before you record it. No bot announces Superwhisper, so tell the other attendees.

## Enterprise controls

Admins set these in the dashboard. See [Configuration & Models](/enterprise/configuration).

- **Allow cloud AI models**: off limits meetings to local models and your organization's custom models
- **Allow system audio recording**: off turns Meetings off
- **Meeting sharing**: public links, sign-in links, or no links
- **Recording retention**: deletes old meetings from members' Macs, like dictations
