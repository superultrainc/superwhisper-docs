Meetings can run fully on your Mac. With a local voice model and a local language model, the audio, transcript, speaker separation, and summary all stay on your device, and Meetings works with no internet connection.

## Set up fully local meetings

1. Open the **Meetings** tab and click the gear to open **Meeting settings**
2. Under **Voice**, choose **Cohere Transcribe**. Parakeet models are a faster option, with lower accuracy
3. Under **LLM**, choose **Tiny Aya 3B** or **Qwen 3.5 2B**
4. Wait for the models to download. After that, meetings need no internet connection

{/* IMAGE: images/screens/mac/meeting-settings.png | Meeting settings with Cohere Transcribe and Tiny Aya 3B selected, and the Notifications group */}

| Model | Role | Size | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cohere Transcribe | Voice | 1.3 GB | The most accurate local transcription, in 14 languages |
| Tiny Aya 3B | LLM | 2.1 GB | Summaries in 60+ languages, from Cohere |
| Qwen 3.5 2B | LLM | 1.3 GB | Faster summaries on smaller Macs |

Cohere Transcribe needs an Apple silicon Mac. Summaries from small local models are shorter and less detailed than cloud summaries. See [local summary tips](/meetings/summaries#local-summaries-are-short-or-miss-the-format).

## Where meeting data goes

| Data | Fully local | With cloud models |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audio | Stays on your Mac | Sent to the voice model provider for transcription |
| Speaker separation | On your Mac | On your Mac |
| Transcript and private notes | Stay on your Mac | Sent to the language model provider to write the summary |
| Summary | Written on your Mac | Written by the language model provider, saved on your Mac |
| Calendar events | Read on your Mac | Read on your Mac |
| Saved meetings | On your Mac | On your Mac |

Superwhisper's cloud models run under zero-data-retention agreements. Providers don't keep your audio or text, and they don't train on it. See [Cloud models: privacy commitments](/security/sensitive-data#cloud-models-privacy-commitments).

You can mix the two. For example, a local voice model with a cloud language model keeps audio on your Mac and sends only the transcript and notes.

### Data that leaves your Mac only when you choose

- **Share links**: [creating a link](/meetings/share#share-a-link) uploads the summary text, title, and date. Links expire after 30 days
- **Agents**: an [AI agent](/meetings/share#ask-an-agent-about-your-meetings) you connect reads meetings, including transcripts and private notes, and sends them to its own provider
- **Files you drag or copy**: the receiving app handles them under its own terms

Superwhisper never uploads meeting audio or transcripts to its own servers.

## Recording consent

Laws in many places require consent from everyone on a call before you record it. Superwhisper doesn't join the call or announce itself, so tell the other attendees when you record.

## Enterprise controls

Enterprise admins can set these policies in the dashboard. See [Configuration & Models](/enterprise/configuration).

- **Allow cloud AI models**: off forces meetings onto local models and your organization's custom models
- **Allow system audio recording**: off turns Meetings off for every member
- **Meeting sharing**: allow share links for anyone, require sign-in to your organization, or turn links off
- **Recording retention**: deletes meetings older than the limit from each member's Mac, the same as dictations
