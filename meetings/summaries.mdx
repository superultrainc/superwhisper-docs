When a recording ends, the language model chosen under **LLM** in [Meeting settings](/meetings/overview#meeting-settings) reads the transcript and your private notes and writes a summary. The text appears as it's written.

{/* IMAGE: images/screens/mac/meeting-summary.png | A finished meeting summary with Decisions and Action items checkboxes */}

## What a summary contains

The built-in **Summary** template writes notes for someone who missed the meeting:

- **Title** and a three to five sentence overview
- **A section per topic**, with the facts and the reasons behind them
- **Decisions**: what was agreed, and why
- **Open questions**: what is still unresolved
- **Action items**: checkboxes with the owner's name, like `- [ ] @Sam send the revised quote`

The summary uses the main language spoken in the meeting. A meeting in Spanish gets a summary in Spanish.

Your [private notes](/meetings/record#take-private-notes) count as facts about the meeting. Use them to correct a name, add context the call didn't cover, or say what matters most to you.

You can edit the title and summary text in the meeting window. Click an action item's checkbox to mark it done.

## Templates

A template is the set of instructions the language model follows when it writes the summary. A template changes only the instructions. The voice model, language model, and recording settings stay the same.

Superwhisper includes three templates:

| Template | What it writes |
| --- | --- |
| **Summary** | What was discussed, decisions made, and action items. The default for every meeting |
| **Email follow up** | A follow-up email to the attendees, ready to send, with a subject line, recap, and next steps |
| **Action items** | Decisions and commitments, with a minimal recap |

### Switch templates

Click the template button in the meeting window toolbar. It shows the template in use, **Summary** by default. Pick another template from the list.

{/* IMAGE: images/screens/mac/meeting-template-menu.png | The template menu open in the meeting toolbar, with Regenerate, All templates, and New template */}

Each meeting keeps every version you generate. Switching back to a template you already ran shows its summary at once. A template you haven't run yet generates a new version from the same transcript.

To pick a template before a meeting starts, use the arrow next to **Record** in the [meeting prompt](/meetings/record#start-a-recording).

Choose **Regenerate** in the same menu to write the current template's summary again, for example after you add notes or rename speakers.

### Write your own template

1. Click the template button in the toolbar and choose **New template**
2. Give the template a name
3. Write the instructions: what to include, the structure, the tone, and who the summary is for
4. Save it, then pick it in the toolbar to run it on the current meeting

Ideas for templates:

- **Client call notes**: requirements, budget, objections, and next steps
- **Interview scorecard**: the candidate's answers by topic, with strengths and concerns
- **Stand-up**: what each person did, what they'll do next, and what blocks them
- **One-on-one**: topics, feedback given, and commitments from each side

For more on writing instructions, see [prompting tips](/modes/custom#prompting-tips).

### Manage templates

Choose **All templates** in the template menu to open **Summary Templates**. From there you can:

{/* IMAGE: images/screens/mac/meeting-templates.png | The Summary Templates window listing the built-in and starter templates */}

- **Edit** any template's instructions, including the built-in **Summary**. An unedited built-in template gets improvements from app updates
- **Star** templates to show them in the toolbar menu. Templates without a star stay in **All templates**
- **Rename** or **delete** your own templates

Templates are saved on your Mac with your settings.

<Info>
If you used a custom meeting mode before Superwhisper 3.0 (a mode with system audio and speaker separation on), Superwhisper copied its instructions into a template. Find it in **All templates**.
</Info>

## Summary problems

### "Not enough was said in this meeting to summarize."

The meeting has fewer than about 25 words and no private notes. Superwhisper doesn't write a summary for it. Check the transcript: if the other side of the call is missing, see [The other side of the call is missing](/meetings/record#the-other-side-of-the-call-is-missing).

### The summary shows the raw transcript

The language model failed, so Superwhisper saved the transcript in its place. Check your internet connection for cloud models, or that a local model finished downloading. Then choose **Regenerate** in the template menu. See [LLM processing errors](/common-issues/llm-processing-error).

### Names in the summary are wrong

The summary uses speaker names from the transcript. Rename speakers with [Assign speakers](/meetings/record#name-speakers), then choose **Regenerate**.

### Local summaries are short or miss the format

Small local models follow long instructions less closely than cloud models. Keep custom templates short and specific. For long meetings in many languages, **Tiny Aya 3B** gives better results than **Qwen 3.5 2B**.
