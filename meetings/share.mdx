## Share a summary

Click **Share** in the meeting toolbar. It shares the summary for the template you're viewing.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/mac/meeting-share-sheet.png" alt="The Share summary sheet with the draggable note, app buttons, and the share link row" />
</Frame>

- **Drag**: drag the note card into any app. It arrives as a Markdown file named after the meeting
- **Open in an app**: click **Slack**, **Microsoft Teams**, **ChatGPT**, **Claude**, **Obsidian**, **Notion**, **Notes**, **Mail**, or **Gmail**, or search for another app. The app opens with the note ready to drop in
- **Copy the file**: right-click the note card and choose **Copy file**
- **Copy text**: the **More** menu (**…**) in the meeting window has **Copy summary** and **Copy transcript**

### Share a link

Click **Click to create link**, then **Copy share link**. Anyone with the link sees the summary on a Superwhisper web page.

- **Uploaded**: the summary text, title, and date only. Audio, transcript, and private notes stay on your Mac
- **Expires**: after 30 days
- **Who can view**: anyone with the link. On an Enterprise license, choose **Everyone at** your organization (sign-in required) or **Anyone with the link**, if your admin allows it

**Sharing is turned off for your organization** means your admin disabled links. See [Meeting sharing](/enterprise/configuration#meeting-sharing).

## Ask an agent about your meetings

Connect an AI app to ask questions across meetings, like "What did we decide about pricing last week?" The app reads your meetings through Superwhisper's MCP server.

### Connect an agent

1. In the **Meetings** tab, click the gear and scroll to **Agents**
2. Turn on an app from the list: **Claude**, **ChatGPT**, **Cursor**, **Claude Code**, or **Codex CLI**. Only apps installed on your Mac appear
3. Restart the app if it's open

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/mac/meeting-settings-agents.png" alt="The Agents group in Meeting settings with Claude and ChatGPT connected" />
</Frame>

Superwhisper installs its [CLI](/get-started/cli) if needed and adds itself to the app's MCP settings. Star an app to make it the default. For other MCP apps, click **MCP command** under **Don't see your app?** and paste the settings into that app.

To ask about one meeting, open the **More** menu (**…**) in the meeting window and choose **Talk with**.

### What the agent can read

The agent can list, search, and read meetings: summaries, action items, private notes, attendees, and transcripts. It can't change or delete them.

<Warning>
Meeting content an agent reads, including transcripts and private notes, goes to that agent's AI provider under the provider's terms. Don't connect an agent if a meeting must stay on your Mac.
</Warning>

### Read meetings from the terminal

The [Superwhisper CLI](/get-started/cli) reads the same meetings:

```bash
superwhisper meetings                       # Recent meetings, newest first
superwhisper meetings search "roadmap"      # Search meetings
superwhisper meetings read                  # Latest meeting: summary, notes, attendees
superwhisper meetings read --transcript     # Include the full transcript
```
