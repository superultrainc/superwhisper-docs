A meeting recording captures your microphone and your Mac's audio together. You follow a live transcript while you talk, and the summary starts when the recording ends.

Before your first meeting, finish the [setup](/meetings/overview#set-up).

## Start a recording

Start a meeting in any of three ways:

- **From a prompt**: when [meeting detection](/meetings/overview#meeting-detection) is on, click **Record** in the **Meeting detected** prompt
- **From your calendar**: with [calendar integration](/meetings/overview#calendar-integration) on, click **Join & Record** or **Record** in the reminder
- **By hand**: open the **Meetings** tab and click **+** (**New recording**). Use this for any meeting app, or for an in-person meeting

The arrow next to **Record** in a prompt lets you pick a [summary template](/meetings/summaries) before the meeting starts.

The meeting window opens and the meeting shows at the top of the **Meetings** tab under **Happening now**.

<Note>
Meetings has no keyboard shortcut. Your dictation shortcut still works during a meeting. While you dictate, your voice stays out of the meeting recording.
</Note>

## During the meeting

### Follow the live transcript

Click **Transcript** in the toolbar to show or hide the transcript panel. It shows **Listening…** until someone speaks. Use **Search transcript or speakers** at the top of the panel to find a word or a person.

Transcripts follow the language of the room. If speakers switch languages, the transcript switches too. To pin one language, set **Language** in [Meeting settings](/meetings/overview#meeting-settings).

### Take private notes

Type in the notes area of the meeting window: **A space for private notes about your meeting.** Your notes guide the summary: the language model treats them as facts about the meeting.

Private notes stay on your Mac. They aren't included when you [share a link](/meetings/share#share-a-link).

### Name speakers

Superwhisper separates speakers on your Mac and labels them **Speaker 1**, **Speaker 2**, and so on. When you record alone with no call audio, your lines show as **You**.

In Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Slack huddles, Superwhisper reads names from the call window and suggests them. To change a name:

1. Click **Assign speakers** (the people icon) in the transcript panel
2. Listen to a sample of each speaker
3. Click a name and type a new one. The name changes everywhere in the transcript

If you give two speakers the same name, Superwhisper asks **Merge with …?** and combines them into one speaker.

### Pause, minimize, and switch models

- **Pause**: click **Pause recording** in the toolbar. Click **Resume recording** to continue
- **Minimize**: press **Cmd-M** or click the yellow window button. The window shrinks to a small pill and recording continues. Click the pill to bring the window back
- **Change the voice model**: you can change **Voice** in Meeting settings during a meeting. If the current model streams, the change applies to your next meeting

Muffle and Lower don't run while a meeting records, so you keep hearing the call at full volume. See [Sound while recording](/get-started/essential-settings).

## End a recording

Click **End recording** (the stop button) in the toolbar. Superwhisper asks **End the recording?** Click **End recording** to stop and start the summary.

Superwhisper also notices when the call ends. It shows **Meeting ending in 9s** and counts down. Click **End meeting** to stop now, or open the menu and choose **Keep recording** to continue.

Pressing **Cmd-W** while recording asks the same question. It never closes the window or drops the recording on its own.

When the recording ends, the summary appears as it's written. See [Summaries and templates](/meetings/summaries).

## Discard a recording

Click the trash button (**Discard this recording**) in the toolbar, then **Discard recording**. Superwhisper deletes the audio and transcript and closes the window. You can't undo this.

## Find past meetings

The **Meetings** tab lists every meeting, newest first, grouped by day. Click a meeting to open it. You can play the audio, edit the title, summary, and notes, and switch templates.

Type in **Search meetings** to search titles, summaries, notes, attendees, and speakers. Add filters to narrow results:

| Filter | Example | Finds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `attendee:` (or `with:`) | `with:alex` | Meetings with Alex invited |
| `speaker:` | `speaker:sam` | Meetings where Sam spoke |
| `template:` | `template:email` | Meetings summarized with a template |
| `date:` (or `on:`) | `on:yesterday` | Meetings on a day: `today`, `yesterday`, `7d` (last 7 days), or a date like `2026-10-15` |
| `after:` / `before:` | `after:2026-10-01` | Meetings in a date range |
| `duration:` | `duration:>15m` | Meetings longer than 15 minutes |

{/* IMAGE: Meetings tab search with a filter */}

The **More** menu (**…**) in the meeting window has **Copy summary**, **Copy transcript**, **Recording info**, and **Delete recording**.

## Where meetings are stored

Meetings are saved on your Mac in the same recordings folder as your dictations. Each meeting keeps its audio, transcript, summary, and notes. The **Keep recordings for** setting in **Settings → Configuration** applies to meetings too, so a short retention period deletes old meetings.

## Troubleshooting

### "Set up meeting models in the Meetings tab first"

The voice or language model for meetings isn't ready. Open the **Meetings** tab and click **Set up meetings**, or pick models in [Meeting settings](/meetings/overview#meeting-settings).

### "No microphone available."

macOS has no input device Superwhisper can use. Connect a microphone or select one in the [input device menu](/common-issues/microphone-and-system-audio#superwhisper-uses-the-wrong-microphone).

### "Meetings are disabled by your organization."

Your Enterprise admin turned off system audio recording, which also turns off Meetings. Ask your admin.

### The other side of the call is missing

Superwhisper can't record your Mac's audio. Turn on **Screen & System Audio Recording** for Superwhisper. See [System audio recordings come out empty](/common-issues/microphone-and-system-audio#system-audio-recordings-come-out-empty-or-silent).

### No "Meeting detected" prompt

1. Check that **Recording suggestion** is **Notify** or **Auto** in Meeting settings
2. Check that the call is in Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or a Slack huddle. For other apps, start the recording by hand
3. On the Free plan, check that you have free words left. Prompts stop when they run out
