Finish the [setup](/meetings/overview#set-up) before your first meeting.

## Start a recording

- **From a prompt**: click **Record** in the **Meeting detected** prompt. See [Meeting detection](/meetings/overview#meeting-detection)
- **From a calendar reminder**: click **Join & Record** or **Record**. See [Calendar integration](/meetings/overview#calendar-integration)
- **By hand**: in the **Meetings** tab, click **+**. Use this for any other app or an in-person meeting

To pick a [summary template](/meetings/summaries) before the meeting starts, click the arrow next to **Record** in a prompt.

The meeting window opens, and the meeting appears under **Happening now** in the **Meetings** tab.

## During the meeting

### Live transcript

Click **Transcript** in the toolbar to show or hide the transcript. Search it with **Search transcript or speakers**.

The transcript follows the language spoken, even when it changes mid-call. To pin one language, set **Language** in [Meeting settings](/meetings/overview#meeting-settings).

### Private notes

Type in the notes area of the meeting window. The summary treats your notes as facts about the meeting. Notes stay on your Mac and aren't part of a [share link](/meetings/share#share-a-link).

### Speaker names

Speakers show as **Speaker 1**, **Speaker 2**, and so on. When you record alone with no call audio, your lines show as **You**. In Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Slack huddles, Superwhisper suggests names from the call.

To rename a speaker:

1. Click **Assign speakers** (the people icon) in the transcript panel
2. Play a sample to hear who it is
3. Click the name and type a new one

The new name replaces the old one everywhere in the transcript. Give two speakers the same name to merge them.

### Pause, minimize, and switch models

- **Pause**: click **Pause recording**, then **Resume recording** to continue
- **Minimize**: press **Cmd-M**. The window shrinks to a small pill and keeps recording. Click the pill to bring it back
- **Switch voice model**: change **Voice** in Meeting settings. If the current model streams, the change applies to your next meeting

Muffle and Lower are off while a meeting records, so you hear the call at full volume.

## End a recording

Click **End recording** (the stop button), then confirm. The summary starts right away. See [Summaries and templates](/meetings/summaries).

When the call ends, Superwhisper shows **Meeting ending in 9s**. Click **End meeting** to stop now, or choose **Keep recording** from its menu.

{/* IMAGE: images/screens/mac/meeting-ending-prompt.png | The Meeting ending in 9s prompt with the End meeting button */}

**Cmd-W** asks before it ends a recording. It never closes the window on its own.

## Discard a recording

Click **Discard this recording** (the trash button), then **Discard recording**. This deletes the audio and transcript and can't be undone.

## Find past meetings

The **Meetings** tab lists meetings by day, newest first. Open one to play the audio, edit the title, summary, or notes, or switch templates. The **More** menu (**…**) has **Copy summary**, **Copy transcript**, **Recording info**, and **Delete recording**.

**Search meetings** looks through titles, summaries, notes, attendees, and speakers. Narrow it with filters:

| Filter | Example | Finds meetings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `attendee:` or `with:` | `with:alex` | With Alex invited |
| `speaker:` | `speaker:sam` | Where Sam spoke |
| `template:` | `template:email` | Summarized with a template |
| `date:` or `on:` | `on:yesterday` | On `today`, `yesterday`, `7d` (the last 7 days), or a date like `2026-10-15` |
| `after:` / `before:` | `after:2026-10-01` | In a date range |
| `duration:` | `duration:>15m` | Longer than 15 minutes |

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/mac/meetings-search.png" alt="Search meetings with a speaker filter applied" />
</Frame>

Meetings are saved on your Mac with your dictations. **Keep recordings for** in **Settings → Configuration** deletes old meetings too.

## Troubleshooting

### "Set up meeting models in the Meetings tab first"

The meeting models aren't ready. In the **Meetings** tab, click **Set up meetings**.

### "No microphone available."

Connect a microphone, or [select one](/common-issues/microphone-and-system-audio#superwhisper-uses-the-wrong-microphone).

### "Meetings are disabled by your organization."

Your Enterprise admin turned off system audio recording, which turns off Meetings.

### The other side of the call is missing

Turn on **Screen & System Audio Recording** for Superwhisper. See [System audio recordings come out empty](/common-issues/microphone-and-system-audio#system-audio-recordings-come-out-empty-or-silent).

### No "Meeting detected" prompt

1. Set **Recording suggestion** to **Notify** or **Auto** in Meeting settings
2. Check that the call is in Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or a Slack huddle. For other apps, start the recording by hand
3. On the Free plan, check that you have free words left
