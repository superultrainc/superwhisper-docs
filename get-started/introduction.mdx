## Key Features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Intelligent Modes">
    Choose from built-in modes such as pure voice transcription, tailored text formatting, or create your own custom modes with personalized AI instructions. Each mode processes your dictation differently - from simple voice-to-text to complex transformations that match your exact requirements and workflow preferences.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Context-Aware AI">
    When enabled, Superwhisper's context awareness understands your specific needs and delivers smarter, more personalized results.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="File Transcription">
    Aside from dictation, Superwhisper can transform existing audio and video files into text. Perfect for processing lectures, meetings, or interviews.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Language Support">
    Dictate in your preferred language and translate to English on the fly, use built-in modes in your language, or create custom modes to harness Superwhisper's capabilities across 100+ supported languages.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use Your Own API Keys">
    Superwhisper's flexibility extends beyond our built-in models. Integrate your own API keys to access additional AI providers and models, expanding your capabilities even further.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Unlimited AI">
  Experience unlimited access to powerful cloud & local AI models from leading providers like OpenAI, Anthropic, Deepgram, and Groq. Process dictations with advanced language models without worrying about usage limits or additional costs.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
New users receive 15 minutes of free access to all pro features and the ability to create 3 modes to experience Superwhisper's full potential.
</Note>
