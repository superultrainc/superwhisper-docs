Superwhisper is a privacy-first dictation app for professionals. Dictate into any text field and get clean, formatted text that sounds like you: messages, emails, notes, and code. It records and summarizes meetings too, and with local models nothing you say leaves your device.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dictation">
      Speak normally (or _whisper_).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transcription">
    State-of-the-art AI models convert your voice into text in milliseconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Smart Processing">
    Optionally, AI enhances your dictation to match your preferred tone, format, and communication style.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Intelligent Modes">
    Use built-in modes for plain transcription or formatted text, or create custom modes with your own AI instructions. Each mode processes your dictation differently, from plain voice-to-text to a full rewrite in your format.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Meeting Recorder">
    Record meetings, including system audio from calls, and get a structured summary with key decisions and action items. Optional [speaker separation](/modes/speaker-separated-meetings) labels who said what.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Write for Me">
    Describe what you need and the **Write for me** mode drafts it: dictate a rough outline and get finished text.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Context-Aware AI">
    When enabled, context awareness uses what you're working on (selected text and clipboard content) to sharpen results.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="File Transcription">
    Superwhisper also transcribes existing audio and video files: lectures, meetings, interviews.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Language Support">
    Superwhisper supports 100+ languages. Dictate in yours, translate to English on the fly, or use any mode, built-in or custom, in your language.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Coding Agents">
    Connect [Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, or Pi](/get-started/coding-agents). Superwhisper pops up when your agent needs input, and you answer by voice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use Your Own API Keys">
    Add your own API keys to use models from other providers alongside the built-in ones.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Unlimited AI">
  Pro includes unlimited cloud and local models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Deepgram, and Groq, with no usage limits or extra costs.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
New users get 3000 words of free access to all Pro features.
</Note>
