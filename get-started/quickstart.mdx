---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Install Superwhisper and complete your first dictation in about three minutes — on macOS or Windows."
---

Install Superwhisper, grant two permissions, and dictate your first text. This takes about three minutes.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="macOS">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Download and install">
        Download Superwhisper from [superwhisper.com](https://superwhisper.com). Open the downloaded file and drag Superwhisper into your **Applications** folder, then launch it.

        <img
          style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
          src="/images/screens/mac/onboarding-welcome.png"
          alt="Superwhisper welcome screen shown on first launch"
        />
      </Step>

      <Step title="Grant permissions">
        Superwhisper asks for two permissions on first launch:

        - **Microphone** — required to record your voice
        - **Accessibility** — required to paste transcribed text into other apps

        <img
          style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
          src="/images/screens/mac/onboarding-permission-mic.png"
          alt="Superwhisper onboarding screen showing microphone and accessibility permissions granted"
        />

        <Note>
        If you skipped a permission, grant it later in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone / Accessibility**.
        </Note>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Follow the setup wizard">
        The onboarding wizard walks you through choosing your language, picking a transcription model (cloud or local), and testing your microphone. The suggested defaults work well — you can change everything later. See [Choose your model](/get-started/choose-your-model) for guidance.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Dictate your first text">
        Click into any text field, press the recording shortcut (**⌥ Space** by default, shown during onboarding), speak, and press it again to stop. Your words are transcribed and pasted at the cursor.

        <img
          style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
          src="/images/screens/mac/recording-window-recording.png"
          alt="Superwhisper recording window showing an active waveform while dictating"
        />
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Windows">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Download and install">
        Download the Windows installer from [superwhisper.com/windows](https://superwhisper.com/windows) and run it. Superwhisper starts automatically and adds an icon to your system tray.

        <Note>
        If the app fails to launch with a missing DLL or Vulkan error, see [Superwhisper on Windows](/get-started/windows#installation-problems).
        </Note>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Grant microphone access">
        Allow microphone access when Windows asks. You can check this later in **Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Follow the setup wizard">
        The onboarding wizard walks you through choosing your language, picking a transcription model (cloud or local), and testing your microphone. The suggested defaults work well — you can change everything later. See [Choose your model](/get-started/choose-your-model) for guidance.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Dictate your first text">
        Click into any text field, press the recording shortcut shown during onboarding, speak, and press it again to stop. Your words are transcribed and pasted at the cursor.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
