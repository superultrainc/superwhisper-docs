---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Install Superwhisper on macOS 14 or later, or Windows 10 or 11. Start dictating in less than a minute."
---

Superwhisper runs on macOS 14 or later and on Windows 10 or 11. Pick your platform below.

<Note>
If your company provides Superwhisper, follow [Activate Your Enterprise License](/enterprise/activate) first to sign in with your work email. Then come back here for your first dictation.
</Note>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="macOS">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Download and install">
        Superwhisper requires **macOS 14 or later**. Download it from [superwhisper.com](https://superwhisper.com), open the file, and drag Superwhisper into your **Applications** folder, then launch it.

        <img
          style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
          src="/images/screens/mac/onboarding-welcome.png"
          alt="Superwhisper welcome screen shown on first launch"
        />
      </Step>

      <Step title="Grant permissions">
        Superwhisper asks for permissions on first launch. Click **Allow** next to each one:

        - **Microphone**: to record your voice
        - **Accessibility**: to insert your dictation into apps
        - **System audio** (optional): to record audio from meeting apps for [Meetings](/meetings/overview). Skip it if you only dictate

        <img
          style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
          src="/images/screens/mac/onboarding-permission-mic.png"
          alt="Superwhisper onboarding screen showing microphone and accessibility permissions granted"
        />

        <Note>
        If you skipped a permission, grant it later in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone / Accessibility / Screen & System Audio Recording**.
        </Note>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Test your microphone and pick a shortcut">
        Say a few words and check that the waveform moves. Then choose your dictation shortcut: a key you hold while you talk. **Right ⌘** is the default. Click **Change shortcut** to pick another key.

        Superwhisper sets up your first mode for you, a Message mode with cloud models. You don't choose a model during setup. To change models later, see [Create your first mode](/get-started/choose-your-model).
      </Step>

      <Step title="Dictate your first text">
        Setup ends with a practice message. Click the message field, hold your shortcut, speak, and let go. Superwhisper transcribes your words and pastes them at the cursor.

        After setup, dictate in any app the same way. You can also press **⌥ Space** (Option + Space) once to start recording and again to stop.

        <img
          style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
          src="/images/screens/mac/recording-window-recording.png"
          alt="Superwhisper recording window showing an active waveform while dictating"
        />
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Windows">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Download and install">
        Download the Windows installer from [superwhisper.com/windows](https://superwhisper.com/windows) and run it. Superwhisper starts automatically and adds an icon to your system tray.

        <Note>
        If the app fails to launch with a missing DLL or Vulkan error, see [Superwhisper on Windows](/get-started/windows#installation-problems).
        </Note>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Grant microphone access">
        Allow microphone access when Windows asks. You can check this later in **Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Follow the setup wizard">
        The wizard asks for your language, transcription model, and microphone. The defaults work well, and you can change everything later.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Dictate your first text">
        Click into any text field, press the recording shortcut shown during onboarding, speak, and press it again to stop. Superwhisper transcribes your words and pastes them at the cursor.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
