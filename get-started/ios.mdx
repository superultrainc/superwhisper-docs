On iPhone, Superwhisper works as a **custom keyboard**: switch to it in any app, dictate, and it inserts your text where you're typing.

## Set up the keyboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Download [Superwhisper from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/superwhisper/id6471464415) and open it once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the keyboard">
    Go to **iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard…** and select **Superwhisper**.

    <img
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
      src="/images/screens/ios/keyboard-settings.png"
      alt="iOS Keyboards settings with the Superwhisper keyboard added"
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow Full Access">
    Tap the Superwhisper keyboard in the same list and turn on **Allow Full Access**. The keyboard needs this to transcribe audio and insert text.

    <img
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
      src="/images/screens/ios/keyboard-full-access.png"
      alt="Allow Full Access toggle for the Superwhisper keyboard in iOS Settings"
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dictate

1. In any app, touch and hold the **globe key** and choose **Superwhisper**

<img
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
  src="/images/screens/ios/keyboard-globe-picker.png"
  alt="iOS keyboard picker opened from the globe key with Superwhisper listed"
/>

2. Tap the record button and speak

<img
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
  src="/images/screens/ios/keyboard-recording.png"
  alt="Superwhisper iOS keyboard recording with an active waveform"
/>

3. Stop the recording. Superwhisper inserts the text at the cursor

<img
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
  src="/images/screens/ios/keyboard-in-use.png"
  alt="Superwhisper iOS keyboard ready to record inside Messages"
/>

<Warning>
**On iOS 26.4 and later**, tapping a Superwhisper button that opens the main app no longer returns you to your previous app. Swipe back instead. This is an Apple platform change affecting all third-party keyboards. Details and status: [Adapting to iOS 26.4](/common-issues/ios-26-keyboard-changes).
</Warning>

## Modes and settings on iOS

The iOS app has its own settings for modes, models, vocabulary, and stats. Open the app and tap the gear icon.

<img
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
  src="/images/screens/ios/settings-home.png"
  alt="Superwhisper iOS app settings screen"
/>

<Note>
Modes and vocabulary are stored per device. They don't sync from your Mac or PC yet. Cloud sync is planned. Recreate your most-used modes in the iOS app's Modes settings.
</Note>

## Keyboard not working

- Confirm **Allow Full Access** is on (iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Superwhisper)
- If the keyboard disappears from the list after an iOS update, re-add it with the steps above
- If dictations fail or vanish, check your connection; cloud models need internet. Then retry from the main app, where recordings appear in History
- If you're still stuck, email [support@superwhisper.com](mailto:support@superwhisper.com) with your iPhone model, iOS version, and Superwhisper version
