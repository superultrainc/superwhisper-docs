On iPhone and iPad, Superwhisper works as a **custom keyboard**: switch to it in any app, dictate, and your transcribed text is inserted where you're typing.

## Set up the keyboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Download [Superwhisper from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/superwhisper/id6471464415) and open it once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the keyboard">
    Go to **iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard…** and select **Superwhisper**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow Full Access">
    Tap the Superwhisper keyboard in the same list and turn on **Allow Full Access**. The keyboard needs this to transcribe audio and insert text.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dictate

1. In any app, touch and hold the **globe key** and choose **Superwhisper**
2. Tap the record button and speak

<img
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}

  alt="Superwhisper iOS keyboard recording with an active waveform"
/>

3. Stop the recording — your transcribed text is inserted at the cursor

<img
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
  src="/images/screens/ios/keyboard-in-use.png"
  alt="Superwhisper iOS keyboard ready to record inside Messages"
/>

<Warning>
**On iOS 26.4 and later**, tapping a Superwhisper button that opens the main app no longer returns you to your previous app automatically — you need to swipe back manually. This is an Apple platform change affecting all third-party keyboards. Details and status: [Adapting to iOS 26.4](/common-issues/ios-26-keyboard-changes).
</Warning>

## Modes and settings on iOS

The iOS app has its own settings for modes, models, vocabulary, and stats — open the app and tap the gear icon.

<img
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
  src="/images/screens/ios/settings-home.png"
  alt="Superwhisper iOS app settings screen"
/>

<Note>
Modes and vocabulary are currently stored per device — they don't sync from your Mac or PC yet. Cloud sync is planned. Recreate your most-used modes in the iOS app's Modes settings.
</Note>

## Keyboard not working?

- Confirm **Allow Full Access** is on (iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Superwhisper)
- If the keyboard disappears from the list after an iOS update, re-add it with the steps above
- If dictations fail to transcribe or vanish, check your connection — cloud models need internet — and try again from the main app, where recordings appear in History
- Still stuck? Email [support@superwhisper.com](mailto:support@superwhisper.com) with your iPhone model, iOS version, and Superwhisper version
