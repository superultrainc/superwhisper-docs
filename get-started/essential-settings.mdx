## Open settings

There are four ways to access the Superwhisper settings:

1. Click the **Superwhisper icon in the menu bar** (near the Wi-Fi icon) and choose **Settings**
2. Right-click the **mini recording window** and choose **Open Settings**
3. Press **⌘ ,** while the app is active (requires "Show in Dock" in [advanced settings](/get-started/settings-advanced#interface-customization))
4. Open the deep link `superwhisper://settings` from any launcher or automation

<Note>On Windows, click the Superwhisper icon in the system tray.</Note>

## Keyboard shortcuts

Open **Settings → Configuration**. 

<img
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
  src="/images/screens/mac/configuration-1.png"
  alt="Superwhisper Configuration pane showing keyboard shortcut settings"
/>

- **Toggle Recording**: Press to start/stop recording.
- **Push-to-Talk**: Press-and-hold to record, release to stop. A quick tap acts as toggle.
- **Cancel Recording**: Cancels the recording without transcribing. Cancels immediately for short recordings; longer recordings ask for confirmation.
- **Change Mode**: Press-and-hold to open the mode changer, then tap the key or use arrows to cycle through modes. Modes can be changed before or during a dictation.
- **Mouse Shortcut**: Assign a mouse button: quick click toggles, press-and-hold acts as push-to-talk. Note that some mice can't detect sustained holds.

<Warning>
**Global vs. mode-specific shortcuts.** The global shortcuts above start recording using the active mode. Separately, each mode has its own **Keyboard shortcut** that starts a recording *in that specific mode*. If a shortcut triggers the wrong mode, check whether it's assigned in both places.
</Warning>

## Sound and microphone

Open **Settings → Sound**.

<img
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
  src="/images/screens/mac/sound.png"
  alt="Superwhisper Sound settings showing input device and sound effect options"
/>

- **Input Device (top right)**: pick a microphone or leave it on automatic to follow the system default. You can also switch input from the [menu bar icon](/get-started/interface-menu-bar).
- **Automatic volume adjustment**: recommended; sets your microphone level for clear recordings.
- **Silence Removal**: recommended; helps prevent hallucinated text during periods of silence.
- **Sound Effects**: adjust the audio cues for recording start and stop. Adjust their volume independently or turn them off.

The active input device also shows in the **top-right of every settings pane** — click it to switch microphones:

  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}

  alt="Superwhisper input device selector open from the top-right of the settings window"
