## Overview
The recording window gives you control and real-time feedback during dictation. This page walks through each component.

---
## Main recording window
### 1. Resize toggle

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem'}}
src="/images/get-started/interface-rec-window-002.png"
alt="Superwhisper Resize Toggle"
/>
When you hover over the recording window, you can click here to switch between the main and mini view.

### 2. Audio waveform

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem'}}
src="/images/get-started/interface-rec-window-004.png"
alt="Superwhisper Audio Waveform"
/>

The waveform shows the audio your microphone captures in real time. A moving waveform means your microphone is working.

If the waveform stays still:
- Verify your selected input device in [Superwhisper's Sound tab](essential-settings#sound-and-microphone)
- Confirm microphone permission in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone**

<Note>Exception: with [system audio capture](../modes/custom#audio-options), the waveform stays still by design. Your audio still records. Verify it in [History](history).</Note>

### 3. Status indicator

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem'}}
src="/images/get-started/interface-rec-window-005.png"
alt="Superwhisper Status Indicator"
/>

The color-coded dot reflects the current recording status, similar to the one in [the menu bar](interface-menu-bar#status-indicator-dot):
- Yellow: Model loading in progress
- Blue: Dictation processing
- Green: Processing completed

### 4. Mode display

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem'}}
src="/images/get-started/interface-rec-window-006.png"
alt="Superwhisper Mode Display"
/>

This area shows your active mode and the keyboard shortcut for switching. Click it to switch modes manually.

<Info>
In Super Mode, this area displays your current application or website context.
</Info>

### 5. Context capture

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem'}}
src="/images/get-started/interface-rec-window-007.png"
alt="Superwhisper Context Capture Indicator"
/>

This indicator appears in Super Mode and in Custom modes with [context awareness](../common-issues/context) enabled. It lights up when Superwhisper captures clipboard context (text copied within 3 seconds before dictation or during it) and when selected text is captured at the moment recording begins.

### 6. Stop recording

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem'}}
src="/images/get-started/interface-rec-window-008.png"
alt="Stop Superwhisper's dictation"
/>

Click here to stop recording, or press the same keyboard shortcut you used to start.

### 7. Cancel dictation

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem'}}
src="/images/get-started/interface-rec-window-009.png"
alt="Cancel Superwhisper's dictation"
/>

Click here to cancel the current dictation, or use your assigned keyboard shortcut. Recordings under 30 seconds cancel immediately; longer ones show a confirmation to prevent accidental cancellation.

## Mini recording window

The mini recording window is a compact version with the same core controls.

### Always-active mini window

In [advanced settings](settings-advanced#recording-window), you can keep the mini window visible even when idle, as a small always-accessible indicator.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hover over the indicator">
    Hover over the mini window to show a control panel with three options:
  1.  Change Mode
  2.  Start Recording
  3.  Expand to Regular Window
  
    <img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem'}}
src="/images/get-started/interface-rec-window-010.png"
alt="Superwhisper Mini Window on Hover"
/>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-request controls">
    The same controls appear after a dictation completes, particularly when results aren't automatically pasted into the front application.

        <img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem'}}
src="/images/get-started/interface-rec-window-011.png"
alt="Superwhisper Mini Window with Resuls"
/>

  </Step>
</Steps>

### Recording with the mini window
Start recording either way:
- Click the recording button in the mini window
- Use your standard keyboard shortcut

During recording, the mini window shows the audio waveform like the main window does (1). Hover over it while recording (2) to show a stop button that ends the recording and starts processing.

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem'}}
src="/images/get-started/interface-rec-window-012.png"
alt="Superwhisper Mini Window During Recording"
/>


### More mini window options

<Tip>
Right-click the mini window for a context menu with Expand Window, Open Settings, and Open History. These options also work during an active recording.
</Tip>
