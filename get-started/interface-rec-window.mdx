The recording window gives you control and real-time feedback during dictation.

<img
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
  src="/images/screens/mac/recording-window-main.png"
  alt="Superwhisper recording window with numbered callouts for each control"
/>

1. **Resize toggle**: switch between the main and mini view
2. **Waveform**: shows the audio your microphone captures in real time
3. **Mode display**: your active mode. Click it to switch modes, or hold **⌥ ⇧ K** (Option + Shift + K). In Super Mode, this area shows your current application or website context
4. **Stop**: ends the recording and starts processing. Click it or press the same keyboard shortcut you used to start
5. **Cancel**: discards the dictation. Click it or press **esc**. Recordings under 30 seconds cancel immediately; longer ones ask for confirmation

## While recording

A moving waveform means your microphone is working:

<img
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
  src="/images/screens/mac/recording-window-recording.png"
  alt="Superwhisper recording window showing an active waveform while dictating"
/>

If the waveform stays still:

- Verify your selected input device in [Superwhisper's Sound tab](/get-started/essential-settings#sound-and-microphone)
- Confirm microphone permission in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone**

<Note>Exception: with [system audio capture](/modes/modes#advanced-settings), the waveform stays still by design. Your audio still records. Verify it in [History](/get-started/history).</Note>

In Super Mode and Custom modes with [context awareness](/common-issues/context) enabled, an indicator lights up when Superwhisper captures clipboard context (text copied within 3 seconds before dictation or during it) or selected text at the moment recording begins.

## Mini recording window

The mini window is a compact version with the same core controls. Click the resize toggle to switch to it.

- **Keep it on screen**: Superwhisper can keep the mini window visible as a small indicator. Hover over it to change mode, start recording, or expand to the regular window
- **While recording**: the mini window shows the waveform; hover to reveal a stop button
- **Right-click** the mini window for a context menu with Expand Window, Open Settings, and Open History
