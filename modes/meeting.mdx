## Key features

Meeting Mode records and summarizes meetings. It:

- Converts spoken discussions into structured summaries
- Highlights key decisions and action items
- Preserves important context while removing redundant content

<Check>Use it for team meetings, client calls, and any conversation you need to review or follow up on.</Check>

## Speaker separation

By default, Meeting Mode doesn't separate speakers in the transcript. Enable speaker separation in the [Advanced Settings sidebar](modes#advanced-settings). Speaker labels appear in the [Segments view only](../get-started/history#speaker-separation); they aren't included in AI-generated summaries or action items.
