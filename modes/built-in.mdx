Superwhisper ships with ready-made modes, each tuned for one task. Create one from **+ Create mode** and pick its preset. This page covers the built-in presets; [Super](/modes/super) and [Custom](/modes/custom) have their own pages.

## Message

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
src="/images/screens/mac/modes-editor-cloud.png"
alt="Superwhisper's Interface for Message Mode"
/>

Message Mode applies AI processing to your dictation. It:

- Removes speech artifacts and filler words
- Fixes grammar and punctuation
- Formats text for readability
- Keeps your natural conversation flow

<Check>Unlike the transcription-only [Voice to Text](#voice-to-text) mode, Message Mode refines your speech while preserving your tone and communication style.</Check>

## Email

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
src="/images/modes/modes-email-001.png"
alt="Interface of Superwhisper's Email Mode"
/>

Email Mode formats your dictation as an email. It:

- Adds greetings and closings
- Keeps your natural tone and style
- Highlights key action items
- Fixes grammar and punctuation

## Note

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
src="/images/modes/modes-note-001.png"
alt="Superwhisper's interface for Note Mode"
/>

Note Mode structures your dictation as a note. It:

- Improves clarity
- Formats text for readability
- Highlights key points and action items
- Adapts the structure to the topic
- Creates lists and bullet points from your dictation

<Check>Note Mode works well for lectures, meetings, brainstorming, and quick thought capture.</Check>

## Meeting

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
src="/images/modes/modes-meeting-001.png"
alt="Superwhisper's Interface for Meeting Mode"
/>

Meeting Mode records and summarizes meetings. It:

- Converts spoken discussions into structured summaries
- Highlights key decisions and action items
- Preserves important context while removing redundant content

By default, Meeting Mode doesn't separate speakers in the transcript. Enable speaker separation in the [Advanced Settings sidebar](/modes/modes#advanced-settings). Speaker labels appear in History's Segments view only; they aren't included in AI-generated summaries or action items. See [Speaker-Separated Meetings](/modes/speaker-separated-meetings) for the full workflow.

</Card>

## Voice to Text

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
src="/images/screens/mac/modes-editor-voice.png"
alt="Superwhisper's interface for Voice Mode"
/>

Voice to Text is transcription only, with no AI processing, so it gives the fastest results. The voice model does all the work; match it to your use case:

  <Card title="Quick dictation" icon="bolt" horizontal>
    - **S1-Voice (Cloud)**: fast and the most accurate model we ship, in 100+ languages.
    - **Parakeet (Local)**: the fastest local option for short dictations (English only; Parakeet Multilanguage for other languages).
    - **Cohere Transcribe (Local)**: the most accurate local model. Nothing you dictate leaves your device.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Longer recordings" icon="stars" horizontal>
    - **Nova (Cloud)**: recommended for long recordings and the best choice for speaker separation.
    - **Other models**: all models can process long recordings, with varying speed and accuracy.
  </Card>

The [Voice models](/models/voice) page lists every model's languages, speed, accuracy, and size.

<Tip>For grammar correction or content refinement, use [Message](#message), [Note](#note), or a [Custom mode](/modes/custom).</Tip>
