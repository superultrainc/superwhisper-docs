A mode pairs a voice model with optional AI processing to shape your dictation for a purpose: plain text, a message, an email, a note, or your own custom workflow. This page covers the settings every mode shares.

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}

alt="Superwhisper's Interface for Modes"
/>

<Tip>Create several versions of the same mode with different voice models, AI models, or settings. Click any mode's name to rename it.</Tip>

## Per-mode settings

### Voice processing
After you dictate, your audio first goes through voice processing for transcription.
<Steps>

<Step title="Set your language">
- Supported languages vary by voice model
- Based on your chosen language and voice model, you may see an option to translate your dictation to English. For best results with this option, use a Whisper model like Pro or Standard

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
src="/images/modes/modes-002.png"
alt="Superwhisper's Interface for Language Selection"
/>

<Note>If your voice model doesn't support translation, or you need a language other than English, set up translation with AI processing in a [Custom mode](custom).</Note>
</Step>

<Step title="Pick your voice model">
Manage all your models in the Advanced Settings sidebar, where you can:
- Download voice models for local use
- Connect your own API tokens for supported services
- Select from the available cloud-based models

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
src="/images/modes/modes-003.png"
alt="Superwhisper's Interface for LLM Selection"
/>

</Step>
</Steps>

### Auto-activation rules
In the Advanced Settings sidebar, set up rules to switch modes automatically based on your active apps and websites.

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
src="/images/modes/modes-004.png"
alt="Auto-Activation Rules interface for setting up automatic mode switching based on apps and websites"
/>

<Note>Once a mode activates for an app or site, you can't override it, and the system doesn't switch back to your previous mode automatically.</Note>

---
### Advanced settings
Below the auto-activation rules, you can select how your audio is handled during dictation:

* **Mute Audio While Recording**
* **Pause Media While Recording**
* **Record from System Audio**
  - Useful for meetings, video content, interviews, or live audio coming through your speakers
  - When recording system audio, the waveform on the recording window doesn't move. Check [History](/get-started/history) to verify capture
* **Identify Speakers**
  - Useful for meeting transcriptions
  - Speaker separation results appear in the Segments tab of History

<Note>Speaker-separated transcripts won't process through AI automatically within the same mode. See [Speaker-Separated Meetings](speaker-separated-meetings) for handling multi-speaker transcripts with AI processing.</Note>

---
### AI processing
After transcription, AI processing shapes your text based on the mode's purpose.

<img
style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
src="/images/modes/modes-005.png"
alt="AI Processing settings panel displaying model selection and language processing options"
/>

- Manage AI models in the same Advanced Settings sidebar, above the selected voice model
<Note>Select a mode with AI processing to see this option.</Note>
- Built-in modes support multiple languages automatically
   - Select the correct language in the [voice processing](#voice-processing) settings
   - The AI processes your dictation and responds in your chosen language
- AI processing varies by mode
   - [Voice to Text](built-in#voice-to-text) has no AI processing
   - The [built-in modes](built-in) (Message, Email, Note, Meeting) and [Super](super) use instructions tuned for their purpose
   - [Custom Mode](custom) lets you write your own AI processing instructions
   - [Super](super) and [Custom](custom) modes add context awareness
