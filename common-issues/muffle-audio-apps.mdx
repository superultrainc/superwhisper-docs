---
title: "Muffle and other audio apps"
description: "Fix silent, doubled, or unmuffled audio when you use Muffle with audio apps like FineTune, SoundSource, or eqMac. Set up the app in one click, or use Lower."
---

**Muffle** keeps just the beat and bass of your music under your voice while you dictate. Choose it in **Settings → Sound → Sound while recording**. It needs macOS 14.2 or later and the **System Audio Recording** permission. See [Sound while recording](/get-started/sound-while-recording) for all the options.

## Why other audio apps get in the way

Some apps route every app's audio through themselves, the same way Muffle does. When both run, they can capture each other:

- Your audio goes silent until you switch the **Sound while recording** option.
- Your audio plays twice, so it sounds doubled or echoey. This is easiest to hear on headphones.

While Muffle is selected, Superwhisper checks whether one of these apps is running and avoids the problem for you. What happens next depends on the app:

| App | What happens |
|---|---|
| FineTune, SoundSource | Muffle works after a quick setup. Until then, Superwhisper uses **Lower**. |
| Background Music, eqMac, Boom 3D, Sound Control | Muffle doesn't work. Superwhisper uses **Lower** instead. |
| Vorssaint, Fader, MacMix, Audio Hijack, Loopback | Not tested yet. Muffle still runs, but might not work well. |

**Lower** turns your audio down while you dictate.

## Set up FineTune or SoundSource

Muffle works with FineTune and SoundSource once that app is told to leave Superwhisper alone. Superwhisper can do this for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sound settings">
    Open **Settings → Sound** in Superwhisper and set **Sound while recording** to **Muffle**. While FineTune or SoundSource is running, a box under the option says **Muffle needs a quick setup with** and the app's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Set up">
    Superwhisper quits the app, adds itself to the list of apps it leaves alone, and opens the app again. It then checks that the app really leaves Superwhisper alone. The box shows **Setting up…** while this happens, usually for 5 to 10 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the confirmation">
    The box changes to **Muffle is now set up with** and the app's name, then disappears after a few seconds. Muffle now works normally.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Set up** restarts FineTune or SoundSource. It opens again on its own, even if the setup doesn't work.
</Note>

If the box says **Couldn't set it up automatically. Follow the steps instead.**, click **Set up** to try again, or follow the manual steps for your app below. **Learn how** in the box opens this page.

## Set up FineTune by hand

Hide Superwhisper in FineTune.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Keep Sound settings open">
    Open **Settings → Sound** in Superwhisper with **Muffle** selected, and leave it open. FineTune only lists apps that are playing audio. While this page shows the setup note, Superwhisper stays listed in FineTune.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open FineTune">
    Click FineTune in the menu bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the app list">
    Click the pencil button at the top, next to the gear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hide Superwhisper">
    Click the eye next to Superwhisper.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Superwhisper shows **Muffle is now set up with FineTune.** Muffle then works normally, and your FineTune volume and EQ still apply.

## Set up SoundSource by hand

Add Superwhisper to SoundSource's **Excluded Applications**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open SoundSource">
    Click SoundSource in the menu bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open its settings">
    Click the gear at the top right, then click **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Audio tab">
    Open the **Audio** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exclude Superwhisper">
    Under **Excluded Applications**, click **Add Exclusion** and choose Superwhisper.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Superwhisper shows **Muffle is now set up with SoundSource.** This can take a few seconds.

<h2 id="apps-that-dont-work-with-muffle">Apps that don't work with Muffle</h2>

These apps reroute your apps' audio the same way Muffle does, and they have no way to leave Superwhisper alone. Muffle can't run alongside them:

- Background Music
- eqMac
- Boom 3D
- Sound Control

While one of them runs and Muffle is selected, **Settings → Sound** shows a warning box that says **Muffle doesn't work with** and the app's name, and Superwhisper uses **Lower** instead. **Learn more** in the box opens this section. To use Muffle, quit the app. Otherwise, keep using Lower.

<h2 id="apps-we-havent-tested-yet">Apps we haven't tested yet</h2>

Muffle still runs alongside these apps. While one of them runs and Muffle is selected, a note in **Settings → Sound** says Superwhisper isn't sure Muffle works well with it. **Learn more** in the note opens this section.

- Vorssaint
- Fader
- MacMix
- Audio Hijack
- Loopback

If your audio cuts out while you dictate, switch **Sound while recording** to **Lower**.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My audio went silent after dictating">
    In **Settings → Sound**, switch **Sound while recording** to another option, such as **Lower**, then switch it back. If your audio is still silent, quit Superwhisper and open it again. Then check whether one of the apps above is running, and set it up or quit it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see the setup box in Settings → Sound">
    The box only appears while **Sound while recording** in **Settings → Sound** is set to **Muffle**, your current mode uses Muffle too, and FineTune or SoundSource is running. If you only chose Muffle in a mode's settings, set it in **Settings → Sound** as well, then open FineTune or SoundSource.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Set up didn't work">
    The box says **Couldn't set it up automatically. Follow the steps instead.** when the other app doesn't quit, its settings can't be changed, or it doesn't come back set up within about 20 seconds. Click **Set up** again, or follow the manual steps for [FineTune](#set-up-finetune-by-hand) or [SoundSource](#set-up-soundsource-by-hand).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The box says FineTune or SoundSource is still replaying Superwhisper">
    Superwhisper doesn't take the other app's settings at their word. Before Muffle runs alongside FineTune or SoundSource, Superwhisper plays a very quiet, brief tone and listens for it in the other app's output. If the tone comes back, the other app is still rerouting Superwhisper, even though its settings say it isn't. Superwhisper then lowers your audio instead, and the box says **Restart FineTune and Superwhisper to finish setting up Muffle** (or SoundSource). Quit and reopen both apps. If the box is still there, remove Superwhisper from the other app's list and add it again by hand, using the steps for [FineTune](#set-up-finetune-by-hand) or [SoundSource](#set-up-soundsource-by-hand).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Muffle lowers the volume instead, even though FineTune or SoundSource is set up">
    Superwhisper can only run that check while the other app is playing something. Until the check has passed, a dictation lowers your audio instead of muffling it. Start your music or video, and the next dictation uses Muffle. This can happen for the first dictation after you open Superwhisper or the other app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I hear a faint, short, high-pitched tone">
    That is the check described above. It plays for under a second when Superwhisper first sees FineTune or SoundSource set up, including when Superwhisper opens, and again if the first check couldn't hear anything. Most people don't notice it. It doesn't play while no such app is running, or while **Sound while recording** is set to something other than Muffle.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My audio sounds doubled or echoey with FineTune or SoundSource">
    Update Superwhisper with **Check for Updates** in **Settings → Configuration**. On older versions, hiding or excluding Superwhisper in the other app stops the silence, but your audio can still play twice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Muffle isn't available or does nothing">
    Muffle needs macOS 14.2 or later. It also needs the **System Audio Recording** permission. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording** and turn on Superwhisper.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

If the problem continues, email [support@superwhisper.com](mailto:support@superwhisper.com) with your app version, your macOS version, and the audio apps you use.
