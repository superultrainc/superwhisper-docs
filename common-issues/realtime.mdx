## Overview
Realtime transcription shows your words on screen as you speak, so you get immediate feedback on accuracy. It works only with voice models that support streaming:

- **Nova** (cloud): Nova 3, Nova 2, and Nova Medical. These need an internet connection
- **Scribe** (cloud, ElevenLabs): needs an internet connection
- **Parakeet** (local): the Parakeet models run on your device. On Windows, only the two **Parakeet Real-time** models support it. See [Realtime on Windows](#realtime-on-windows)

## Realtime on Windows

These voice models support Realtime on Windows:

- **Nova 3**, **Nova 2**, and **Nova Medical** (cloud)
- **Scribe** (cloud)
- **Parakeet Real-time (English)** and **Parakeet Real-time (Multilingual)** (local)

The two Parakeet Real-time models are experimental. They appear in the model list only when **Settings → AI Models → Show experimental models** is on. Each is a 2.4 GB download. They run through ONNX Runtime, so they don't need Vulkan.

The standard **Parakeet** model on Windows doesn't support Realtime. Cohere Transcribe, the Whisper models, and S1-Voice don't support it either.

## Why the toggle is missing or grayed out

- **Voice model**: your mode uses a model that doesn't support streaming, such as Cohere Transcribe or the Whisper models. Switch to one of the models above. On Windows, see [Realtime on Windows](#realtime-on-windows).
- **License**: Realtime needs a Pro license.
- **Identify Speakers**: for Scribe and Nova models, **Realtime** and **Identify Speakers** can't be on together. Turn **Identify Speakers** off to use Realtime, or keep it on and leave Realtime off.

## Enable realtime transcription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the mode">
    Open Superwhisper and go to the mode you want to edit
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select a realtime-capable voice model">
    In the mode editor, set **Voice Model** to a Nova, Scribe, or Parakeet model
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Realtime">
    With a realtime-capable model selected, the **Realtime** toggle becomes available. Turn it on

      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}

  </Step>
</Steps>
