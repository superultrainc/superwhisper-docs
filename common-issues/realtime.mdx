## Overview
Realtime transcription shows your words on screen as you speak, so you get immediate feedback on accuracy. It works only with voice models that support streaming:

- **Nova (Cloud)**: requires an internet connection
- **Parakeet Realtime (Local)**: runs on your device and works offline

If the Realtime toggle is missing or grayed out, your mode uses a voice model that doesn't support streaming. Switch to one of the models above.

---
## Enable realtime transcription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the mode">
    Open Superwhisper and go to the mode you want to edit
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select a realtime-capable voice model">
    In the mode editor, set **Voice Model** to a **Nova** model (cloud) or a **Parakeet** realtime-capable model (local)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Realtime">
    With a realtime-capable model selected, the **Realtime** toggle becomes available. Turn it on

    <img
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
      src="/images/screens/mac/modes-editor-voice.png"
      alt="Superwhisper mode editor with a Parakeet model selected and Realtime enabled"
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>
