## Overview
Realtime transcription shows your words on screen as you speak, so you get immediate feedback on accuracy. The feature is **only supported by the Nova (Cloud) voice models**. If the toggle is missing or grayed out, your mode uses a different voice model.

---
## Enable realtime transcription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the mode">
    Open Superwhisper and go to the mode you want to edit
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select a Nova voice model">
    In the Advanced Settings sidebar, open the **Voice model** section and select a **Nova** model from the dropdown
    
    <img
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
      src="/images/common-issues/realtime-002.png"
      alt="Superwhisper Voice Models"
    />

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Realtime">
    With a Nova model selected, the Realtime toggle becomes available. Turn it on
    
    <img
      style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
      src="/images/common-issues/realtime-003.png"
      alt="Activating Realtime in Superwhisper"
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>
