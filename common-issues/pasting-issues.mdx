---
title: "Text Not Pasting, Pasting Twice, or Pasting Old Text"
description: "Fix Superwhisper paste problems: results that only appear in History, doubled text, or previous clipboard content appearing instead."
---

Superwhisper delivers results by pasting at your cursor. When pasting goes wrong, the transcription itself is fine: it's in [History](/get-started/history) and on your clipboard. Each delivery problem below has a specific fix.

## Nothing pastes and the text is only in History

1. **Grant Accessibility permission** (macOS): open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility** and enable Superwhisper. The app needs this permission to paste into other apps, and it's the most common cause.
2. **Keep focus in the target field**: Superwhisper pastes at the active cursor. If you click elsewhere while it processes, the paste has no target. The text stays on your clipboard; paste it manually.
3. **Some apps block synthetic pasting**: secure fields, some terminals, and VMs. Enable **Simulate Keypresses** in [advanced settings](/get-started/settings-advanced). It types the result key by key instead.

## Old clipboard content pastes instead

This comes from **Restore Clipboard** in [advanced settings](/get-started/settings-advanced). It puts your pre-dictation clipboard back after pasting. If the paste is slow or the target app reads the clipboard late, the app picks up the restored content instead of your dictation. Turn **Restore Clipboard** off if this happens to you.

## Text pastes twice

This usually means two delivery paths fired at once:

- If **Simulate Keypresses** is on and the target app also accepts the paste, you can get both. Turn Simulate Keypresses off for apps that paste normally.
- If you press the shortcut twice while processing, both dictations paste in sequence. Check History to confirm what happened.

If neither applies, email [support@superwhisper.com](mailto:support@superwhisper.com) and name the app it happens in. Paste behavior is app-specific, and reports that name the app get fixed fastest.

## Auto-send fires when you didn't want it

**Hold Shift to Auto-Send** simulates Return after pasting when you finish a dictation holding Shift. If messages send themselves, you're holding Shift at the end of dictations. Turn the feature off in [advanced settings](/get-started/settings-advanced) if you don't want it.
