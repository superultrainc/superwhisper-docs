## Superwhisper uses the wrong microphone

Click the **microphone button** at the top right of the **Home**, **Configuration**, and **Sound** pages to see and switch the active input device:

<img
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
  src="/images/screens/mac/input-device-dropdown.png"
  alt="Superwhisper input device selector open from the top-right of the settings window"
/>

- **Use system default** follows whatever macOS or Windows has selected, which changes when a Bluetooth headset connects. If Superwhisper switches microphones on its own, the system default changed underneath it.
- **Star a microphone** to mark it as a priority device. Superwhisper prefers starred devices over the others.
- **Exclude a device** with its exclude button in the same list. Superwhisper then stops offering that device, for example a virtual microphone you never use.

## AirPods and Bluetooth headsets sound worse

When a Bluetooth headset's microphone is active, audio quality often drops. This is a Bluetooth limitation, not a Superwhisper setting, and it can reduce transcription accuracy. Try selecting your **built-in microphone** as the input and keep listening through the headset.

## System audio recordings come out empty or silent

System audio is what your computer plays, such as the other side of a call. [Meetings](/meetings/overview) records it automatically. Dictation modes record it only when the mode has it turned on:

1. **Grant the permission**: system audio needs **Screen & System Audio Recording** permission. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording** (called **Screen Recording** on macOS 14) and enable Superwhisper. Quit and reopen the app if macOS asks
2. **Enable it in the mode**: for a dictation mode, edit the mode and turn on **Record from system audio**. It records applications on your main display along with your microphone. See [audio options](/modes/modes#advanced-settings)
3. **A still waveform is normal**: the waveform doesn't move during system-audio capture. Check [History](/get-started/history) to confirm the audio recorded
4. **Check your org policy**: on an Enterprise license, your organization can disable system audio recording org-wide, which also hides Meetings. If the option is missing, ask your admin
5. **Rule out your audio device**: if a meeting recorded silent, retry with built-in speakers or wired headphones. If that works, the device is the cause. If it still fails, email [support@superwhisper.com](mailto:support@superwhisper.com) with the device model and your macOS version

## Notifications and other apps in the recording

System audio capture records everything the computer plays, including notification sounds. Mute notifications during meetings you record, or record microphone-only modes when you don't need the other side.

## Muffle doesn't run during meetings

Muffle and Lower pause while a meeting records, so you hear the call at full volume.

<h2 id="muffle-doesnt-work">Muffle is silent, doubled, or doesn't muffle</h2>

Some apps send all of your Mac's audio through themselves, as Muffle does. When both run, your audio can go silent or play twice. Superwhisper finds these apps and uses **Lower** when Muffle can't run.

Muffle also needs macOS 14.2 or later and the **System Audio Recording** permission. See [the permission step above](#system-audio-recordings-come-out-empty-or-silent).

### Set up FineTune or SoundSource

Open **Settings → Sound** with **Muffle** selected. A box names the app. Click **Set up**. Superwhisper restarts the app and tells it to leave Superwhisper alone. This takes 5 to 10 seconds.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/mac/muffle-setup.png" alt="Sound settings with Muffle selected and the FineTune setup box" />
</Frame>

The box shows only while the current mode uses Muffle and the app is running.

If the box says **Couldn't set it up automatically**, do it by hand:

- **FineTune**: keep **Settings → Sound** open. Click FineTune in the menu bar, click the pencil, then click the eye next to Superwhisper
- **SoundSource**: click SoundSource in the menu bar, then the gear, then **Settings → Audio**. Under **Excluded Applications**, click **Add Exclusion** and choose Superwhisper

After setup, Superwhisper plays a short, quiet tone to confirm the app no longer reroutes it. The check needs the other app to be playing audio. Until it passes, Superwhisper uses **Lower**. If the box says to restart both apps, quit and reopen FineTune or SoundSource and Superwhisper.

<h3 id="apps-that-dont-work-with-muffle">Apps that don't work with Muffle</h3>

Background Music, eqMac, Boom 3D, and Sound Control. While one of them runs, Superwhisper uses **Lower**. Quit the app to use Muffle.

<h3 id="apps-we-havent-tested-yet">Apps we haven't tested yet</h3>

Vorssaint, Fader, MacMix, Audio Hijack, and Loopback. Muffle still runs alongside them. If your audio cuts out, switch to **Lower**.

### Audio stays silent after dictating

In **Settings → Sound**, switch **Sound while recording** to another option and back. If it's still silent, quit and reopen Superwhisper.
