## Superwhisper uses the wrong microphone

The active input device shows in the **top-right of every settings pane**. Click it to switch:

<img
  style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }}
  src="/images/screens/mac/input-device-dropdown.png"
  alt="Superwhisper input device selector open from the top-right of the settings window"
/>

- **Use system default** follows whatever macOS or Windows has selected, which changes when a Bluetooth headset connects. If Superwhisper switches microphones on its own, the system default changed underneath it.
- **Pin a specific microphone** to stop the switching. The star marks your preferred device.

## AirPods and Bluetooth headsets sound worse

When a Bluetooth headset's microphone is active, the audio quality drops. This is a Bluetooth limitation, not a Superwhisper setting, and it reduces transcription accuracy. For best results, pin your **built-in microphone** as the input and keep listening through the headset.

## System audio recordings come out empty or silent

System audio (recording what your computer plays, for meetings) is a per-mode feature:

1. **Enable it in the mode**: edit the mode and turn on **Record from System Audio**. See [audio options](/modes/modes#advanced-settings)
2. **A still waveform is normal**: the waveform doesn't move during system-audio capture. Check [History](/get-started/history) to confirm the audio recorded
3. **Check your org policy**: on an Enterprise license, your organization can disable system audio recording org-wide. If the option is missing, ask your admin
4. **Bluetooth output can complicate capture**: if a meeting recorded silent while using Bluetooth audio, retry with built-in speakers or wired headphones and email [support@superwhisper.com](mailto:support@superwhisper.com) with the device model

## Notifications and other apps in the recording

System-audio capture records everything the computer plays, including notification sounds. Mute notifications during meetings you record, or record microphone-only modes when you don't need the other side.
