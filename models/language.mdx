Language models take your transcribed text and format or transform it according to your mode's instructions. There are two types: cloud models run on hosted servers, and local models run entirely on your device. Usage of every model below is covered by your license, with no API keys needed.

<Note>
Speed is a relative rating from 1 to 10. Our benchmarks cover voice models only; see [How We Benchmark](/models/benchmarks) for what we measure.
</Note>

## Superwhisper (Cloud)

Hosted by Superwhisper and optimized for low latency and high-quality text processing.

| Name        | Speed | Size  | License |
| ----------- | ----- | ----- | ------- |
| S1-Language | 10    | Cloud | Pro     |

## Superwhisper (Local)

S1-mini runs entirely on your device, on macOS and Windows (Windows ARM devices need a cloud model instead). It cleans up English punctuation, numbers, dates, and fillers. It works offline, and with a local voice model nothing you dictate leaves your machine.

| Name    | Speed | Size   | License |
| ------- | ----- | ------ | ------- |
| S1-mini | 10    | 462 MB | Pro     |

S1-mini is English only. It works only in Message and Mail modes, so it can't run Super, Note, Meeting, or Custom modes.

## Legacy models

The app marks these older models as deprecated: GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 mini, GPT-4.1 nano, GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini, GPT-3.5 Turbo, GPT-5.3 Instant, Sonnet 4, Sonnet 3.7, Sonnet 3.5, Sonnet 3, Haiku 3.5, Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite, and Grok 4.1 Fast. Use a current model from the tables above instead.

## Choosing a model

S1-Language is the default and the right choice for most modes. Pick S1-mini when you dictate English in a Message or Mail mode and want the whole pipeline on-device. Reach for a larger model (GPT-5.2, Sonnet 5, Gemini 3.7 Flash) only when a mode does heavy rewriting and quality matters more than speed.

You can also add models from your own providers with your own API keys under **Settings → Models library → Bring your own keys**.
