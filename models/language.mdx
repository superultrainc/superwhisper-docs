Language models take your transcribed text and format or transform it according to your mode's instructions. There are two types: cloud models run on hosted servers, and local models run entirely on your device. Usage of every model below is covered by your license, with no API keys needed.

## Superwhisper (Cloud)

Hosted by Superwhisper and optimized for low latency and high-quality text processing.

## Choosing a model

S1-Language is the default and the right choice for most modes. Pick S1-mini when your mode uses a local voice model and you want the whole pipeline on-device. Reach for a larger model (GPT-5, Claude 4.5 Sonnet) only when a mode does heavy rewriting and quality matters more than speed.

You can also add models from your own providers with your own API keys under **Settings → Configuration → Advanced settings → AI Models**.
