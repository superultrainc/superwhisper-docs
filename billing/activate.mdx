After purchase, your license key arrives by email from superwhisper.com. Enter it once per device. The same key works on macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android.

      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your key">
        Type or paste your license key into the key boxes. Keep the uppercase lettering.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open your license settings">
        Click the Superwhisper tray icon to open settings, then open the **Superwhisper** license panel.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your key">
        Type or paste your license key. Keep the uppercase lettering. The same key from a Mac purchase works here; there is no separate Windows license.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="iOS">
    **From a Mac (QR code):**
    1. On your Mac, open Superwhisper's settings and click the **Superwhisper** button (bottom-left) to show your license details and the mobile activation QR code
    2. Scan the QR code with your iPhone

    **From Windows, or manually:**
    1. Find your license key (Windows app settings, or your purchase email)
    2. On your iPhone, open Safari and enter:
       ```
       superwhisper://license/YOUR-KEY
       ```
    3. Tap **Open** when prompted

    **Or type it in:** open the Superwhisper iOS app → **Settings → Superwhisper Pro** → enter your key.

    <Note>
    If you bought Pro inside the iOS app, your key is at **Settings → Superwhisper Pro → Reveal License Key**. Use it to activate Mac and Windows.
    </Note>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open the Superwhisper app, tap the gear icon, then tap your plan at the top of **Settings**
    2. Tap **Already have a license?**
    3. Choose **Scan QR Code** and scan the code shown in your Mac's license panel, or choose **Enter License Key** and type the key from your purchase email

## Find or recover a lost license key

The same steps recover any license type: Monthly, Annual, or Lifetime. Lifetime keys never expire, so a recovered key activates immediately.

1. **Search your email** for a message from `superwhisper.com`. Check spam and promotions folders, and every address you might have used at checkout
2. **Bought on the App Store**: open the iOS app → **Settings → Superwhisper Pro** → **Recover my License Key**
3. **Bought on Google Play**: open the Android app → gear icon → your plan → **Already have a license?** → **Recover License Key**
4. **Still nothing**: email [support@superwhisper.com](mailto:support@superwhisper.com) with the receipt or the email you purchased with, and we'll resend your key. For a Lifetime license, mention the original purchase email address even if you no longer have access to it

## Activation troubleshooting

**The QR code won't scan.** Copy your key on the Mac (double-click it; you'll see "Copied to clipboard"), then open `superwhisper://license/YOUR-KEY` on your iPhone in Safari.

**The app still shows a trial after I entered my key.** Check for extra spaces, keep the uppercase lettering, and make sure you're entering the key emailed by superwhisper.com, not an order number. If it still fails, email [support@superwhisper.com](mailto:support@superwhisper.com) with your key and purchase email.

**App Store purchase not recognized.** Open the iOS app → **Settings → Superwhisper Pro** → **Recover my License Key**. This re-checks your App Store purchase and reactivates the subscription.

**Google Play purchase not recognized.** Open the Android app → gear icon → your plan → **Already have a license?** → **Recover License Key**. Confirm you're signed in to the Google account that made the purchase.

**On a company (Enterprise) license**: you don't need a key. Follow [Activate Your Enterprise License](/enterprise/activate). To move a device off an org, use **Unlink device** in the license panel.
